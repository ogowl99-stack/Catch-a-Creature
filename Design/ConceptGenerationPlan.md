# Concept Generation Plan

## Purpose

This plan defines the minimum visual package needed to understand the complete game environment before Roblox Studio and Blender production begins. It avoids generating decorative art that does not resolve a design or construction decision.

Truth label: Not yet implemented

Status as of 2026-07-17: Research and pre-production. No location shown in concept art has been implemented or verified in Roblox Studio.

## Approved foundation

1. Crescent Pond Sanctuary atmosphere and broad spatial direction
2. Welcome Gate with a centered first-arrival sightline to the Memory Tree
3. Equal 72×72-stud player plot direction
4. Southwest Caretaker Hub opening northeast toward the central tree and pond

The approved files are indexed in [`ConceptArt/README.md`](ConceptArt/README.md).

## Remaining visual package

### 1. Top-down master map — concept rendered; dimensions still required

The corrected whole-map reference is stored at [`ConceptArt/Approved/whole-map-top-down-v2.png`](ConceptArt/Approved/whole-map-top-down-v2.png). It shows an eight-plot planning assumption, true crescent water shape, central landmark, southern gate, shared and future-reserved footprints, paths, and an illustrative outer buffer. A deterministic construction overlay must still define every plot center, exact 72×72 boundary, path width, pond and tree footprint, building footprint, tutorial stop, social space, and reserved expansion zone.

### 2. Memory Tree and Crescent Pond gameplay sheet

Show shore access, bridges, seating, gathering capacity, interaction points, event visibility, and safe routes around the central landmark.

### 3. Creature Breeding Sanctuary

Show breeding pens, inherited-trait presentation, mutation feedback, nests or incubation areas, creature transfer flow, and collection display without copying another game's assets or terminology. The northwest space in the whole-map concept is a future reserved footprint; breeding remains deferred until the core loop is proven.

### 4. Market and selling area

Show a fast everyday selling route for flora and creatures, clear price feedback, and enough queue space that players do not congest the Caretaker Hub.

### 5. Fully developed 72×72 plot

Show one representative late-progression plot containing habitat flora, creature space, a breeding footprint, paths, storage, and decoration. Use this to test whether the standardized footprint remains readable at high density.

### 6. Plot customization kit

Create an original modular language for fences, gates, paths, lamps, benches, signs, ponds, planters, habitat props, and a small number of coherent decorative themes.

### 7. Plant and habitat-flora style sheet

Show original plant families, growth stages, harvestables, adapted seeds, and mutation effects. Mutations must communicate through multiple channels such as color, silhouette, material, particles, and motion.

### 8. Creature and lineage style sheet

Show starter creatures, body-family rules, readable trait inheritance, rarity progression, breeding outcomes, and mutations. Designs must remain cute, recognizable at Roblox camera distance, and practical to model and animate.

### 9. Server-wide weather-event concepts

Show ordinary rain through extremely rare magical events, including how each state changes the whole sanctuary while preserving path, plot, plant, and creature readability. The northeast meadow is a future event reserve. Paid weather activation remains a monetization design decision, not an implemented feature.

### 10. Core interface concepts

Cover the HUD, inventory, field journal, breeding flow, weather wheel, shop, selling, and plot-building interface for desktop, mobile, tablet, and controller navigation.

### 11. Lighting and ambience sheet

Establish morning, afternoon, sunset, night, rain, and rare-event lighting. Maintain a cozy base mood, legible interaction surfaces, restrained bloom, and low-fatigue audio-visual feedback.

### 12. Blender construction sheets

After a design is approved spatially, create front, side, rear, top, scale, material, modular-seam, and animation-pivot references for each important building, creature, plant family, and prop kit.

## Production gate

Beauty art alone does not authorize production. Before a concept becomes a final asset:

1. Confirm its gameplay purpose.
2. Confirm its map footprint and dependencies.
3. Graybox it in Roblox Studio.
4. Test camera, navigation, interaction reach, and maximum representative density.
5. Set a measured performance budget.
6. Only then begin detailed Blender or Roblox construction.
