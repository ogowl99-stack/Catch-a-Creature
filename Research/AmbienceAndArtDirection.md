# Ambience and Art Direction

- Date: 2026-07-16
- Status: Provisional art thesis
- Truth label: Not yet implemented

## Target feeling

> A living storybook sanctuary that feels warm, soft, safe, gently magical, and pleasant to inhabit even when the player is only organizing a plot or watching creatures.

Five ambience pillars:

- Cozy
- Cute
- Peaceful
- Alive
- Wonder-filled

## Visual foundation

The base world should stay restrained so player flora, creatures, weather, and customization remain the spectacle.

- Soft sage and spring-green grass
- Warm cocoa soil
- Cream, sandstone, and peach paths
- Honey-brown wood
- Turquoise water
- Cream buildings with pastel roofs
- Lavender, coral, mint, and amber magical accents
- Rounded architecture and readable silhouettes

## Lighting

Daylight should resemble a warm morning after light rain: warm sun, cool blue-green shadows, pastel sky, slow clouds, mild distance haze, and subtle highlights. Avoid harsh white light, crushed black shadows, aggressive bloom, or constant high saturation.

Night should remain cozy and navigable through periwinkle sky, cool moonlight, warm plot lanterns, glowing paths, fireflies, and bioluminescent flora. Rare weather may be dramatic but must preserve readability and avoid flashing or overwhelming effects.

Weather transitions should blend over several seconds with coordinated sky, lighting, audio, particles, plant movement, and creature behavior.

## Map atmosphere

- Compact central sanctuary landmark or pond
- Curving paths and small bridges
- Rounded fences and hedges
- Benches, lanterns, picnic spaces, and resting areas
- Streams and stepping stones
- Plots arranged around a community hub
- Distant hills and oversized foliage enclosing the world
- Small hidden observation spaces
- Clear navigation even when plots become crowded

## Ambient motion

- Irregular grass and leaf sway
- Occasional falling leaves and water ripples
- Slow cloud movement
- Original tiny pollinators and floating spores
- Creature breathing, blinking, stretching, ear, and tail motion
- Plants reacting when creatures touch, sleep, sing, or forage nearby

Motion should be slow and asynchronous. Constant synchronized animation would create visual noise.

## Sound direction

Music should be light and low-fatigue: gentle acoustic instruments, soft piano, wooden percussion, celesta, and restrained strings. Weather should add or remove layers instead of constantly replacing the entire score.

Ambient sound should include wind, water, distant calls, morning birds, nighttime insects, rain on leaves and roofs, and quiet settlement activity. Interaction sounds should be short, warm, recognizable, and leave silence between actions.

## Blender model language

- Rounded, strongly readable silhouettes
- Chunky proportions and soft bevels
- Expressive faces and eyes
- Simplified paws, wings, tails, and plant structures
- Oversized leaves and habitat features
- Clean materials with subtle gradients
- Small asymmetries that feel handmade
- Creature animations that communicate clues and personality

## UI language

The interface should resemble a friendly illustrated field journal: rounded cream panels, soft brown outlines, original hand-drawn icons, page tabs, restrained notification use, and smooth short transitions. It should preserve a large view of the world.

## First ambience validation scene

Before full production, validate one plot, one pond, one large tree, one plant, one creature, day/sunset/night lighting, one rain state, and a small soundscape. Use it to lock palette, scale, lighting, animation density, and performance before creating an asset catalog.

## Approved first weather

Soft Rain is the approved first-playable weather state. It is natural, server-wide light rain with softer and cooler lighting, gentle rain ambience, and water ripples. It has no growth multiplier, rarity boost, paid activation, exclusive creature, or progression requirement. Exact presentation, timing, and measured budgets remain provisional.
