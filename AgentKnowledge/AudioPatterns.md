# Audio Patterns

## Initial direction

- Low-fatigue layered soundtrack
- Gentle acoustic, piano, wooden percussion, celesta, and restrained string palette
- Weather adds/removes layers instead of constantly restarting music
- Spatial ambience for water, wind, wildlife, and settlement zones
- Short recognizable action sounds with silence between them
- Creature voices support personality and gameplay clues
- Throttle repeated harvest, particle, and creature sounds
- Use audio created specifically for Catch a Creature and record its creator, ownership, and provenance

Status: Not yet implemented. Requires audio created specifically for this project, recorded ownership, and Roblox testing.
